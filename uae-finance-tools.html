UAE Finance Calculators

GCC Finance Tools provides simple online finance calculators for common salary, VAT, invoicing and end-of-service calculations in the United Arab Emirates.

UAE Salary Calculator

Use the UAE salary calculator to estimate your net salary after entering salary components, allowances and deductions.

The calculator can help employees and businesses understand salary calculations in the UAE.

UAE VAT Calculator

Use the UAE VAT calculator to calculate VAT and the resulting total amount based on the amount and VAT rate entered.

UAE Invoice Generator

The UAE invoice generator allows businesses to prepare invoices with company, customer, item, payment and tax information.

Completed invoices can be downloaded as PDF files.

UAE Gratuity and End-of-Service Calculator

Use the gratuity or end-of-service calculator to estimate an amount based on the salary and service period entered.
