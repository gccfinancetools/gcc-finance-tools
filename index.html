Frequently Asked Questions

What finance calculators are available?

GCC Finance Tools provides salary, VAT, invoice and gratuity or end-of-service calculators.

Which GCC countries are supported?

The website currently supports Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain.

Is there a Qatar salary calculator?

Yes. Select Qatar from the country selector and use the Salary Calculator to calculate salary amounts based on the information entered.

Is there a Qatar VAT calculator?

Yes. Select Qatar and open the VAT Calculator. The calculator uses the VAT rate associated with the selected country.

Can I create a UAE invoice?

Yes. Select the United Arab Emirates and open the Invoice Generator to prepare an invoice and download it as a PDF.

Can I calculate Saudi Arabia end-of-service gratuity?

The Gratuity / End-of-Service Calculator can be used with Saudi Arabia selected to estimate an end-of-service amount based on the salary and service period entered.

Can I use the tools for Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain?

Yes. The country selector includes Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain, along with Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

Does the invoice generator create a PDF?

Yes. After preparing the invoice, you can use the Download Invoice PDF button to generate the invoice as a PDF.
